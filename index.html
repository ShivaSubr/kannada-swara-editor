Help · ಸ್ವರ ಸಂಕೇತಗಳ ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿ

Octaves: Select ಕೆಳ ಸ್ಥಾಯಿ for a dot below (lower octave), ಮಧ್ಯ ಸ್ಥಾಯಿ for no dot, or ಮೇಲಿನ ಸ್ಥಾಯಿ for a dot above (upper octave), then click a swara.

Deergha: ಸ, adds one duration unit; ಸ; adds two; ಸ,, also adds two. Place the cursor immediately after a button-inserted swara before clicking the duration mark.

Tala: Use | for a division and || for a double division. Space and New line buttons separate phrases.

Automatic saving: Your edits are saved in this browser on this device. It is not cloud sync. Use Save Project to download a backup, and Open Project to resume on another computer. Browser storage can be cleared, so keep JSON backups.

Raga scale: Type the Aarohana and Avarohana in their separate editable lines above the composition. Both are saved and printed.

Printing: Use Print / Save PDF and select “Save as PDF” in the print dialog. The title, raga, tala and composer appear above the swaras. Octave dots are drawn independently of Kannada font combining-mark support.
