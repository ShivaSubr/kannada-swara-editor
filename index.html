Help · ಸ್ವರ ಸಂಕೇತಗಳ ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿ

Octaves: Select ಕೆಳ ಸ್ಥಾಯಿ for a dot below (lower octave), ಮಧ್ಯ ಸ್ಥಾಯಿ for no dot, or ಮೇಲಿನ ಸ್ಥಾಯಿ for a dot above (upper octave), then click a swara.

Lyrics: Click after a swara line and select Add lyrics below swaras. Click in the new lyrics line; swara shortcuts are disabled there. Press Space to convert English-keyboard words. Lyrics can be typed in Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, or English, and are saved and printed. Switching notation language does not transliterate lyrics.

Octave shortcuts: Alt+1 = lower, Alt+2 = middle, Alt+3 = upper. Select an octave, then type S R G M P D N. The octave stays selected until you change it.

Keyboard: In SWARA lines only, S R G M P D N (uppercase or lowercase) insert swaras. In LYRICS lines, these shortcuts are disabled. Other keys retain normal typing. Ctrl+1 / Ctrl+2 / Ctrl+0 apply single / double / no underline to a selected swara or the swara just before the cursor.

Sections: Use the Pallavi, Anupallavi, or Charana buttons to insert Kannada section headings at the cursor. Headings are saved and printed.

Underlines: For one swara use Single/Double underline. For a continuous line under several notes and the spaces between them, drag to select the entire phrase and choose Underline entire sequence or Double-line entire sequence. Underlines are retained in saved projects and printouts.

Phonetic deergha: Type Maa to insert ಮ, (or the selected script equivalent). Similarly Saa, Gaa, Paa, Daa, Rii and Nee. Three repeated vowels extend the note further. You can also use the Duration menu.

Deergha: ಸ, adds one duration unit; ಸ; adds two; ಸ,, also adds two. Place the cursor immediately after a button-inserted swara before clicking the duration mark.

Tala: Use | for a division and || for a double division. Space and New line buttons separate phrases.

Automatic saving: Your edits are saved in this browser on this device. It is not cloud sync. Use Save Project to download a backup, and Open Project to resume on another computer. Browser storage can be cleared, so keep .swr backups.

Raga scale: Select a raga from the library to automatically fill both scales, or type the Aarohana and Avarohana in their separate editable lines above the composition. Use Unicode subscripts ₁ ₂ ₃ for swara variants. Both are saved and printed.

Printing: Use Compact PDF / Print and select “Save as PDF” in the print dialog. Turn OFF “Headers and footers” in Chrome’s More settings to hide the website URL, date, and page title. The title, raga, tala and composer appear above the swaras. Octave dots are drawn independently of Kannada font combining-mark support.
